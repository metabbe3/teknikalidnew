# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Indonesian retail investors and active IDX traders — **active traders primary**, learning investors secondary, served on the same surface.

- **Primary (active traders):** Want pro-grade charts, the full indicator set (RSI, MACD, SMA/EMA, Bollinger, Stochastic, ADX, ATR), a fast screener, paper trading to test ideas, a watchlist, and a community of IDX traders. They tolerate dense data when it earns its place; speed and precision matter more than hand-holding. Context: checking moves during/after IDX hours (09:00–15:00 WIB), often on mobile during the day, on desktop for real analysis.
- **Secondary (learning investors):** Newer to technical analysis. They arrive through berita/akademi content and need plain-language explanations, guided onboarding, and a clear path: read an article → open a chart → understand a signal.

**Job to be done:** make better-informed IDX trading decisions using professional-grade technical analysis — and learn the craft along the way, in Bahasa Indonesia, on an IDX-native surface.

## Product Purpose

TeknikalID is a professional technical-analysis platform for the Indonesia Stock Exchange, focused on the IDX40 (top LQ45 constituents). It exists because Indonesian retail investors lack a free, high-quality, IDX-native alternative to Western tooling: most charting platforms are USD-centric, English-only, and treat the IDX as an afterthought.

It combines near-real-time price data and indicators (Yahoo Finance, ~5–10 min delay), TradingView charts, a screener, paper trading, portfolio tracking, watchlists, and a trader community — wrapped in an editorial publication (berita + akademi) that teaches the craft and drives discovery.

Success: an active trader can screen, chart, read signals, paper-trade an idea, and discuss it without leaving the platform or translating anything — and a newer investor can grow from reader to practitioner on the same surface.

## Brand Personality

**Authoritative, calm, precise.** The voice of a seasoned analyst, not a hype merchant. Three words: *professional, trustworthy, IDX-native*.

- Confidence comes from data and clarity, not loud color or motion.
- Numbers are exact and tabular; the typography earns the trust.
- "Terminal meets broadsheet": the precision and density of a trading terminal, the readability and gravitas of a financial broadsheet (Newsreader serif for editorial).
- Calm, not cold; Indonesian, not Western-transplanted.

Emotional goal: landing should feel like being in the hands of an expert — the same gravity as a Bloomberg terminal or the FT, but tuned for the IDX and in Bahasa Indonesia.

## Anti-references

- **Crypto / gambling-bro hype.** No neon gradients, moon/rocket iconography, or get-rich-quick energy. Trading is a serious analytical discipline, not a casino.
- **Generic SaaS-fintech default.** No cream/beige 2026-AI backgrounds, identical icon-card grids, or the big-number + supporting-stats hero-metric template. The committed terminal+broadsheet identity wins over SaaS fashion.
- **Cluttered legacy broker portals.** No dense, low-contrast, old-school IDX broker UIs crammed with tables. Stay modern, editorial, and breathable; density must earn its place.
- **Western-market templates, verbatim.** Don't copy NYSE/Nasdaq visual conventions wholesale. This is an IDX product for an Indonesian audience — localize conventions and language, don't transplant.

## Design Principles

1. **Precision over polish.** A number that's right and readable beats a number that's pretty. Tabular figures, exact decimals, mono for all financial values. Trust is built in the digits.
2. **Earned density.** Show a lot only where a trader needs a lot (charts, screener tables). Never add density for its own sake; whitespace is a tool, not waste.
3. **Direction is meaning.** Bullish/bearish (teal/red) is data, not decoration. Use it consistently and only where it carries information — never as a generic accent.
4. **Practice what you preach.** The platform teaches technical analysis in akademi; the tools must embody the same rigor. Every signal the UI shows should be defensible by the indicators behind it.
5. **IDX-native, Indonesian-first.** Conventions, language, and context are tuned to the IDX and Bahasa Indonesia. Never a translated Western template.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**. Body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1. The muted stone secondary text must be verified and bumped toward ink wherever it falls short — readable numbers are a trust issue, not a style preference.
- **Color is never the only signal.** Pair bullish/bearish (teal/red) with icons, arrows, or text labels so colorblind users — and the deuteranopia-heavy, male-skewed trading audience — read direction without color.
- **Reduced motion respected.** The ticker tape, pulses, and stagger reveals already ship `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks; preserve and extend them. Financial data should not induce motion sensitivity.
- **Mobile-first Indonesia.** Most users arrive on mid-range Android over mobile data; stay fast and legible at small sizes and on slow connections.
- **Bahasa Indonesia primary**; English only where it's the trader-native term (ticker symbols, indicator names like RSI/MACD).
