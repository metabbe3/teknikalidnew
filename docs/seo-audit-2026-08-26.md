## Content Mix Problem (biggest strategic issue)

Published articles: 864 DAILY_SNAPSHOT, 355 MOVEMENT_ANALYSIS, 20 EDUCATIONAL,
16 STOCK_ANALYSIS.

- Daily snapshots auto-noindex when stale (article-freshness.ts) → content
  evaporates from the index; no compounding.
- Evergreen/pillar content (the kind that ranks for "belajar saham pemula dari
  n0", "cara membaca candlestick", etc.) is ~36 articles total.
- `/belajar-saham` landing page from keyword-research priority list was never
  built (404 today).
